## Why

Inter for everything. Purple-to-blue gradients. Cards nested in cards, gray text on colored backgrounds, a rounded-square icon tile above every heading. Ask an agent for a page with no other guidance and these show up again and again.

- **Words for what you want.** "Make it pop" leaves your agent to guess. `/impeccable bolder the pricing cards` loads a playbook written for that one move and points it at one target.
- **A reviewer.** `critique` scores the design against usability heuristics and ranks what to fix first.
- **A slop detector.** 62 deterministic rules flag the tells above ...
