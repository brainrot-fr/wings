Defects found
WCAG mislabels. a11y-audit calls 2.4.11 “Focus Appearance (AA)”. In WCAG 2.2, 2.4.11 is Focus Not Obscured (AA) and Focus Appearance is 2.4.13 (AAA). Its own quick-ref has it right, so the skill contradicts itself. accessibility-review lists 2.5.5 under AA, but it is AAA.
Large-text threshold. oma’s accessibility and color docs say “18px bold” counts as large text. The WCAG threshold is 14pt bold (≈18.67px).
Contrast tables. I hand-recomputed three color-contrast-guide rows and got about 2.4, 2.7 and 3.5 against the stated 3.06, 2.81 and 3.13. The Tailwind mapping also looks off (gray-600 comes out near 7.6, stated 5.74). The checker script wasn’t sent, so treat these as unverified and generate ratios by script.
Input borders. ui-ux-pro-max’s default input border (gray-300) is about 1.5:1 on white. oma’s dark --border is white at 10%. Both fail 1.4.11 non-text contrast unless another 3:1 cue exists, and both conflate border with input.
Disabled state. states-and-variants sets pointer-events: none and cursor: not-allowed together, so the cursor never shows.
tailwind-integration. Defines no --card variables but maps them. Labels an RGB triplet as HSL. Mixes two naming schemes.
Generated MASTER.md violates its own rules. design_system.py emits transition: all, hover translateY, hardcoded hex, and Inter as the fallback font. Its page-override generator is keyword guesswork.
Provenance. The 40-odd reasoning records are sourced only from sibling CSVs (circular). Their confidence values (0.88 etc.) are unexplained. Under the file’s own 90-day policy they expired about 10 Nov. Some verifiedAt dates also post-date generatedAt.
Duplicated hex in the oma-design DESIGN.md. The Section 9 prompts copy hex values, so tokens have two homes. That breaks the single-owner rule.
spatial-design. Bans non-8px spacing but its own scale contains 4 and 12, and py-40 isn’t on it.
aria-patterns. The “aria-live on a container with content gets announced on load” claim is doubtful, and the real gotcha is the inverse. I wouldn’t carry it.
ECC-style provenance caution applies here too: Vercel’s “mutations <500ms” and oma’s perf figures are unsourced thresholds.
